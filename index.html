About us

We welcome students of any interests to join, as one of our primary goals is to offer a diverse range of weekly sessions, which aim to combine aspects of Psychology with other subjects and interests. Our weekly sessions will range from speaker's events, debates, and crafts to socials, study group sessions, and so many more!

Whether your interest in Psychology is mild or great, we ensure that the best part about being a member of our society is that there is always something for everyone.
